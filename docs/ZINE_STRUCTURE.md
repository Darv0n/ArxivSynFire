## Sections

### ▓▓▓ THE DROP ▓▓▓

*What landed this week that matters.*

5-7 items. Knife commentary. No fluff.

**Format per item:**

```markdown
### [TITLE]
**Source:** [url] | **Type:** [paper/news/repo/patent]

[2-3 sentence summary with actual insight]

**The real story:** [what it means that they're not saying]
```

**Voice:** Direct. Sardonic. "Here's what actually matters about this."

### ▓▓▓ THE CALLS ▓▓▓

*Predictions. Dated. Specific. Accountable.*

No weasel words. Specific enough to be wrong. Dated for accountability.

**Format:**

```markdown
| # | PREDICTION | HORIZON | CONFIDENCE | REASONING |
|---|------------|---------|------------|-----------|
| 1 | [specific claim] | [date] | [X%] | [why] |
| 2 | [specific claim] | [date] | [X%] | [why] |
| 3 | [specific claim] | [date] | [X%] | [why] |

### CALL DETAILS

**CALL #1:** [Expanded prediction]
- **If true:** [implications]
- **If false:** [what it means]
- **Watch for:** [early indicators]
```

**Confidence Calibration:**

- 90%+ = "WILL happen"
- 70-90% = "HIGH confidence"
- 50-70% = "LIKELY"
- 30-50% = "POSSIBLE"
- <30% = "WILDCARD"

## Satirical Voice Bank

**On corporate announcements:**

- "[Company] announced they're 'committed to AI safety' which means their lawyers finally read the news."
- "The press release said 'breakthrough.' The paper said 'incremental improvement.' The code said '404 not found.'"
- "'We're excited to announce' is corporate for 'we had to announce before [competitor] did.'"

**On overhyped papers:**

- "This paper has been cited 400 times by people who read the abstract and 3 times by people who ran the code."
- "Claimed SOTA. On their own benchmark. That they designed. After seeing the test set."
- "Revolutionary if true. If."

**On talent movement:**

- "When the senior research scientists start their own company, they're optimistic. When they join a hedge fund, pay attention."
- "LinkedIn says 'excited for this new chapter.' Reality says 'got a better offer before the layoffs.'"
- "The talent is moving [direction]. The press releases will follow in six months."

**On paradigm shifts:**

- "The field is debating whether [x] counts as AGI while [y] ships production code that makes the question irrelevant."
- "In three years they'll pretend they always knew this. Screenshot this."
- "The future is here. It's just arguing about naming conventions."

**On accountability:**

- "We said [x] would happen by [date]. It happened on [actual date]. We're either lucky or right. Repeatedly."
- "We missed this one. Here's why. No hiding."
- "The graveyard keeps us honest. Check the receipts."
